# Zavija feedback

Do you play [Zavija](https://zavija.com) and have an idea, or did you find something broken? Tell us here.

Everything on this site is public. You can read what other players asked for and how it was handled.

## How to send us an idea or a bug

1. **Sign in.** This site is called GitHub. If you don't have an account, creating one is free and takes a minute
   (the **Sign up** button at the top of the page).
2. **Click here: [Send an idea or report a bug](../../issues/new/choose).**
3. Choose **Idea** or **Bug**, answer the short questions, and press the green button.

## See what other players have posted

**[Read all the posts](../../issues)** (GitHub calls each post an "issue").

If someone already asked for what you want, press the 👍 under their post instead of writing a new one.

## Good to know

- One idea per post, please.
- Write in whatever language you're comfortable with.
- Don't put your email address or account details here, because anyone can read this site. For anything about your
  account or a payment, use the support email inside the app.

## What the coloured tags on a post mean

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `idea` | a player asked for something new |
| `bug` | something doesn't work as it should |
| `in progress` | we're building it |
| `shipped` | it's live on zavija.com |
| `not planned` | we decided against it |
